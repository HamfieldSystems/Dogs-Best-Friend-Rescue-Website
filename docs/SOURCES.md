The Wayback Machine holds 8 pages for the site (Home, About, Membership, Adoption, Sponsor, Get Involved, Donate, Partners), all captured in May–June 2022. All 8 have been recovered. The only things left out are the homepage's "Activities" paragraph ("check here regularly…"), which goes against keeping the site evergreen, the Covid visiting note, the Wix contact forms (replaced with email links) and the Cibes logo.

The Facebook page itself couldn't be read directly (it needs a login), so no recent posts, numbers or stories were copied from it.

The photos were downloaded at full resolution from the rescue's original Wix media library. Wix stock images (debit card, "girl with dogs", "high fives", Unsplash) were left out. Images are optimised to WebP automatically at build time.
